Earth textures — day (Blue Marble), night lights, topology (bump), water (specular).
Source imagery: NASA Earth Observatory (Blue Marble: Next Generation), public domain.
Bundled via the three-globe example asset set (MIT License).
Resized for web/offline use. Credit: "NASA Earth Observatory".

Mars & Sun surface maps: PlanetPixelEmporium (James Hastings-Trew), free to use with credit.
Asteroid surface: lunar map (three.js examples, NASA/USGS-derived). Downsized for web/offline use.

Structure/surface textures (struct-textures.js): ambientCG.com, CC0 (public domain) —
  SolarPanel001 (solar-wing PV cells), MetalPlates006 (capacitor/buffer/converter/drive-module
  casings, spatial-demo crates), Metal032 (radiator fins), Ground031 (cracked ground after the
  body fractures), Concrete033 (spatial-demo floor). No attribution required; credited anyway.

Smoke/vapour particle sprites (surface-assets.js): OpenGameArt.org "Smoke & vapor particles",
  CC0 / public domain. Used for the surface-view dust and vapour columns.

Impact fireball sprite-sheet (fireball.js): procedurally generated original work, CC0.
  NOT CURRENTLY IN USE — no page loads fireball.js. Kept for a future impact effect;
  safe to delete along with this entry if it is not wired in.

All of the above are inlined as data URIs so WebGL textures are not CORS-tainted on file://.
