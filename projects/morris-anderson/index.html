            The First 90 Days

            What a typical first ninety days looks like

            Three phases that map onto the opening stages of our process. Real programmes vary with the site, the planning position and the information available — this is the typical shape, not a promise of dates.

            Day 1Day 14Day 45Day 90
            01Understand
            02Test
            03Prepare

            Days 1–14 · Understand. Site, brief and information reviewed; written summary and an honest recommendation.

            Days 15–45 · Test. Feasibility against planning, access, ground and services; indicative cost plan against budget.

            Days 46–90 · Prepare. Delivery strategy in writing; outline programme, procurement strategy, mobilisation readiness.

            Indicative activity chart — durations and overlaps are agreed per project once the site and design information are known.
