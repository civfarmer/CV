Turnkey Builds

One party accountable from first drawing to handed-over keys

A turnkey build means Morris Anderson carries the whole delivery: planning strategy, design coordination, procurement, programme, trades, statutory inspections and handover. You make the decisions that matter; we carry everything between them.

What it is

Everything between decision and delivery

On a turnkey project there is exactly one accountable party. We take the project from feasibility through planning, technical design coordination, procurement of every trade and material, the construction programme itself, building-control inspections and the final handover pack. You are consulted at every decision that affects cost, appearance or use — and shielded from the several hundred coordination decisions that do not.

Design and planning
Feasibility, planning strategy, architect and engineer coordination, discharge of planning conditions.
Procurement
Trade packages tendered on equal scope, long-lead materials ordered against the programme, delivery scheduling.
Delivery
Site management, sequencing, quality inspection at each stage, statutory building-control inspections booked and evidenced.
Handover
Commissioning, snagging, certificates, warranties and the operation and maintenance information — the complete pack.

Who it suits

Clients who want the outcome, not the second job

  • People building a principal home who cannot — or would rather not — give a build twenty hours a week
  • Clients overseas or relocating during the build, managing everything through the client portal
  • Complex or constrained sites — hillside work, retaining structures, restricted access — where sequencing errors are expensive
  • Anyone who wants a single point of responsibility if something goes wrong, rather than a circle of parties pointing at each other

If you would rather keep control of parts of the build yourself, the supported self-build routes may fit better — the comparison is set out plainly there.

How it is best deployed

Appoint before planning is submitted

A turnkey appointment earns most of its keep before anyone is on site. Appointed early, we shape the planning application around buildability and budget rather than discovering conflicts later, and long-lead procurement starts against a real programme.

  1. Feasibility and budget shapeThe site, the objective and the realistic cost envelope — before design fees are committed.
  2. Planning strategyApplication prepared around what can actually be built well on the site; pre-application advice where it pays.
  3. Technical design and procurementEngineering coordinated, trade packages tendered on equal scope, long-lead items ordered.
  4. ConstructionA levelled programme, inspection at every stage gate, and a live client portal showing exactly where the build stands.
  5. Completion and handoverCommissioning, certificates, warranties and aftercare — the building and its paperwork, finished.
Already have planning permission? Turnkey still works — we join at technical design and carry everything from there. The earlier the appointment, the more risk is priced out rather than discovered.

Cost and timeline

Indicative figures, honestly framed

High-end turnkey new builds are generally priced from around £2,500 per square metre upward, depending on site, specification and access. The build-cost estimator gives a project-specific indicative range in about two minutes and can send the full breakdown to us as a quote request.

A typical detached turnkey build runs roughly 12 to 18 months on site, preceded by planning and technical design. Every live project runs on a levelled programme with delivery-confidence modelling — the same programme clients watch in the portal.
