Self-Build

Keep the control you want. Hand over the risk you do not.

Self-building rewards the disciplined and punishes the optimistic. Our self-build routes let you decide how much of the running you do yourself — with professional management, procurement muscle and regulatory cover underneath whichever share you keep.

The three routes

Match the support to your appetite

Most support

Managed self-build

We run the programme, procurement and site coordination end to end; you keep design authority and every meaningful decision. Closest to turnkey while remaining your build.

Balanced

Guided self-build

You run the day to day; we set the programme, tender the packages, inspect at stage gates and step in where experience matters most. Structured cover without full management cost.

Most independent

Cost-controlled self-build

You manage the build; we provide the cost plan, procurement framework and scheduled reviews that keep it honest. The discipline, hired in.

Indicative pricing for each route is built into the estimator — choose the self-build route that matches and it prices that level of support.

The honest division

What you keep, what we carry

You keep
Design authority, specification choices, supplier preferences you care about, the pace of decisions — and the saving that comes from carrying part of the load.
We carry
Programme and sequencing, tender comparison on equal scope, long-lead procurement timing, building-control choreography, and the record-keeping that protects you.
Shared, explicitly
Cost control — you see committed against forecast line by line; changes are priced before they are agreed, never after.
Never left to chance
Statutory compliance. Whatever the route, inspections are booked at the right moments and evidenced — read the regulation guide.
Thinking of running the whole thing yourself? Genuinely possible — thousands do it well every year. Our plain-spoken guide to doing your own build sets out exactly what the job involves, so you choose with your eyes open.

How it is best deployed

Engage at design stage, not at the first crisis

  1. Before the plot, ideallyA plot appraised before purchase is the cheapest risk decision of the whole project — services, access, ground and planning context.
  2. At design stageThe support route is chosen and the cost plan built alongside the design, so the design is priced as it develops rather than after.
  3. Before ground is brokenProgramme set, packages tendered, long-lead orders placed, building control engaged. The build starts against a plan, not a hope.
  4. During the buildStage-gate inspections and scheduled reviews at minimum; full coordination on the managed route. The portal keeps the record either way.

Self-builders may also qualify for relief such as the Community Infrastructure Levy self-build exemption and VAT rules specific to new dwellings — the rules change and depend on circumstances, so verify the current position with your local authority and HMRC before relying on either.
