Contemporary Hillside Residence

A contemporary ~200 m² timber-frame residence positioned into a hillside, requiring careful coordination of retaining works, restricted access and easement considerations.

The challenge

The site slopes significantly, placing much of the building volume into the hillside. Retaining structures, restricted access and easement considerations all had to be resolved within one coordinated sequence rather than treated as isolated problems.

The approach

The programme was built around the realities of the site: retaining and groundworks sequenced ahead of the frame, deliveries planned around the access constraints, and easement considerations tracked through each stage so they informed decisions rather than interrupting them.

Key considerations

  • Sequencing retaining structures ahead of superstructure work
  • Managing deliveries and plant within restricted access
  • Coordinating groundworks with the timber-frame programme
  • Keeping easement obligations visible throughout delivery
