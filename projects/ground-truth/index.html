three offline instruments no network calls Case study Portfolio

Three instruments that check a claim against the authoritative record, with the network disconnected. Each opens as a self-contained page, carries its own dated corpus, and states what it cannot see as plainly as what it can.

Why offline is the point, not the constraint. These are the conditions the work is actually done in: a network that is unavailable, a network that is not trusted, or a query whose subject is itself the sensitive part — the counterparty you are checking, the authority you are about to cite, the dependency you are about to disclose. An instrument that has to call an API leaks the question even when it answers it correctly, and answers nothing at all when the line is down.

What they refuse to do. Chokepoint will not read a dependency below HS 6-digit resolution and says so rather than guessing. Veritas will not call an authority fabricated because it predates its corpus — a verifier that cries wolf on Donoghue v Stevenson teaches its user to ignore it. Lineage records accounting consolidation, which is not beneficial ownership, and does not pretend to be. In each case the limit is stated in the tool, carried in the data pack, and reproduced on every export.
