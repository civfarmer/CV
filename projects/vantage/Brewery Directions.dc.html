Forecasting Sandbox · Flagship · Brewery & Alcohol Distribution

One forecasting engine. Three skins to choose from.

Each option below runs the identical forecasting engine on the same 3 years of weekly brewery data — Holt-Winters, regression, Croston and baselines, back-tested and auto-selected per SKU. Only the interface changes. That is the point: the maths is the product, and the skin flexes to match the client. Pick a direction and I'll build the full platform on it.

1A · Cellar Ledger — warm, operational, tactile.   1B · Signal Terminal — dark, dense, high-signal.   1C · Northwind Planner — clean modern SaaS. Try each one: change the scenario, or type a recent weekly figure into Enter actual and watch the forecast recalibrate with a plain-English reason.
