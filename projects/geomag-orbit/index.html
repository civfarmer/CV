Interactive 3D · IGRF-14 degree-1 screen

Geomagnetic attitude-control authority — orbit screen

Drag to orbit the Earth. Choose an altitude, inclination and body torque axis, and the panel recomputes the IGRF-14 degree-1 field along one orbit and the fraction of magnetic control authority available to that axis — the screen behind the companion attitude-control paper. The in-browser calculation reproduces the deposited Python screen at every one of its 27 reference cases — nine orbits × three torque axes (checked live, below).

Screening model, not a simulator. Degree-1 (dipole) IGRF-14 at epoch 2025.0 — an auditable screen, not a full degree-13 propagation, disturbance model, structural model, or controller simulation. Authority η is the per-orbit fraction of torque a magnetic actuator can direct along the chosen axis; it says nothing about slew rate, which the paper bounds separately.
