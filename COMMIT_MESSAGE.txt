Restore the title pages the last rebuild dropped; The Interval carries its DOI

The papers' rebuild pipeline had lost the step that merges the title page -
the citation block, status, keywords, licence and date that a law reader
cites from. The last push served both GGE papers without it. Restored
(pandoc --include-before-body, with pandoc's own thin title block turned
off, which is how the originals were built), and both papers now open on
the proper page, dated 17 August 2026.

The Interval, Not the Autonomy additionally carries its DOI on the title
page and in the suggested citation: 10.5281/zenodo.21970778, reserved
today on Zenodo ahead of deposit. A Robot Left in a Field follows when
its DOI is reserved.
