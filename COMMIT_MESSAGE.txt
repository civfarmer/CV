Remove the AI-assistance statements from the portfolio page

Two lines on the home page volunteered that the work was AI-assisted. The
site is the landing point for submissions to Geneva missions, DG GROW and
the Swiss federal process, and a reader who arrives from a letter citing a
DOI should meet the argument, not a note about how it was typed.

  "Architected and built by me - AI-assisted, never AI-designed; the
   analysis engines, data models and interfaces are mine."
      -> "Architected and built by me: the analysis engines, data models
          and interfaces are mine."

  "~20,000 lines of JavaScript, authored by me (AI-assisted with Claude
   Code)."  (inside the FRIS "How it's built" panel)
      -> "~20,000 lines of JavaScript, authored by me."

The ownership claim is unchanged in both. Only the qualifier is gone.

Two skills chips were removed for the same reason: "Claude Code", which
names a code-authoring tool and sat directly above a portfolio of code,
and "Compliance-audit prompting", which reads as the audit being prompted
rather than performed.

Two were deliberately kept. "Agentic-LLM workflows (HITL)" and "GenAI
control mapping" are governance capabilities - they describe overseeing
these systems, which is the opposite claim to being written by one, and
it is the subject of several of the papers. Removing them would cost a
real qualification to solve a problem they do not create.

Nothing was changed in the papers themselves. Anthropic, OpenAI, the AI
Security Institute and the legal-hallucination literature appear in
Papers 2, 3 and 4 and in "Who Pays to Check the Machine" as cited
sources on AI in cyber operations. Those are bibliography, not
provenance, and they stay.
