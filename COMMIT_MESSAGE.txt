The apparatus the law reader looks for, and a conclusion that says what to do

Both GGE papers now close with proper end-matter: a Table of Instruments
and Official Documents and a Bibliography (The Interval), and a Table of
Cases, Table of Instruments and Bibliography (A Robot Left in a Field).
Only works actually cited are listed, and a two-way audit enforced it:
every bibliography entry traces to a footnote, every footnote source
appears in the apparatus. The one mismatch it caught was fixed the OSCOLA
way - the emplaced paper's first reference to its companion now carries
the full citation and the sibling's DOI.

The Interval's summative remarks now end by saying what a reader should
do: a delegation can table the element as a working paper; a ministry can
put the four-line record into targeting doctrine without waiting; and the
question to carry away is the one the framework makes askable - how long
did the authorisation have to hold, how fast did the facts decay, and
could the system be reached when they did.
