Publish the corpus; rebuild the publications index; self-host the typefaces

The publication PDFs had never been deployed. This adds all sixteen of them —
the nine-paper economic warfare series, the seven manuscripts including the
Swiss submission — together with the relationship map, which existed locally
and had never been pushed.

Publications index rebuilt as a list rather than a scroll of cards. Measured in
Chromium at 390px: 20,573px down to 5,287px, 24.4 phone screens to 6.3, while
gaining three works. The height was never the item count — a card cost a median
630px and abstracts alone were 44% of the page. Each work is now a title, one
metadata line, subject tags, and the abstract inside a <details> so it stays in
the source for a crawler and costs nothing in height until wanted.

No domain tabs. Two independent reviews rejected them: the counts do not support
a three-way split, 64% of the corpus is genuinely cross-domain, and Google
Scholar's guidelines exclude pages that require a click to reach an abstract.
Domain is carried as non-exclusive tags instead.

The jump navigation listed four sections and omitted two holding 16 of the 25
documents, including the entire economic warfare series. It now lists all six.
Sections are named by subject rather than by production phase.

Three public-international-law papers on orbital conduct moved out of the
Research Lab, where they sat between a railgun tradespace explorer and a
launcher capstone, into a Space law and orbital conduct section.

Typefaces are now actually present. There was no @font-face rule and no font
file anywhere in the repository, so Zilla Slab, Inter and IBM Plex Mono had been
declared for two years and never shipped — every visitor saw Georgia over Segoe
UI. Eight woff2 files, latin subset, 176KB, self-hosted so the offline-first
constraint holds. Zilla Slab replaced by Source Serif 4, which was drawn for
extended screen reading and has real italics. Reading pages set in it at 18px on
1.62 leading: 60 characters per line rather than 77.

The map was display:none below 760px, so every phone visitor got a heading and
one sentence. It now scrolls sideways at a legible width.

Register: the availability pill and its green dot removed from the 18
publications pages — a journal editor had "Open to legal, risk & transformation
roles" pinned beside 14,000 words on orbital kinetic systems. "War Room" renamed
to "Crisis case studies" across 29 files. Theme now respects the operating
system before first paint, so a deep link from a submission email no longer
lands on dark navy regardless of preference.

Fixes found by rendering rather than reading: projects/meridian/demo.mp4 was
referenced by the home page and did not exist; the master was unused in
_build/video-masters. The Research Lab's descriptions, twelve of which exceed
320 characters and one of which reaches 1,321, now clamp to four lines with a
control to open them, applied by script so no-JS readers keep the full text.

Papers republished after audit: Paper 5 (97 of 104 cross-references pointed at
the wrong footnote; a citation belonging to an unrelated case), Paper 6, Papers
0, 3 and 3B (four fabricated citations in 3B and all fourteen of its
cross-references wrong), and Regulating Robots by Owning Them.

Not published: working sources under publications/_source, the unredacted
reference letters, local dev servers, and unlinked CV variants.
