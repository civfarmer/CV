The two GGE papers, revised to the state of play their audience is living in

Both papers were dated 6 August and cited the rolling text as revised on
8 November 2024. The delegations they are written for sat through a first
reading of the 18 December 2025 status in March and return to it on
31 August. Revised 14 August 2026:

The Interval, Not the Autonomy (34pp): now reads the December 2025 text
part by part - the central formula's migration from prohibition to stated
need between revisions, the scale-of-operation measure that absorbed the
four parameters, the new understanding requirement in part IV that answers
the critics' gap in form and leaves it open in substance. The March session
is in the chronology: first reading, revised boxes I-III, support past
seventy states, and the United States proposing "good faith human judgement
and care" - a live demonstration that the negotiation is a dispute over
which unmeasured words to prefer. The ICRC's October 2025 position paper is
engaged where it comes closest: its "invalidate legal determinations"
passage is the decay of a targeting judgement stated qualitatively, and the
paper now says precisely what it adds - the rate, the record, the
assessment.

A Robot Left in a Field Is a Mine the Law Cannot See (25pp): the novelty
section now names its nearest neighbours honestly (Lewis's Yale LJ model
argument; the ICRC's "indicative baselines") and distinguishes
precedent-as-model from characterisation-plus-amendment. A new subsection
records the corroboration neither drafter intended: the rolling text and
the ICRC both now prescribe the Technical Annex's two-layer deactivation
and perimeter discipline for mobile systems, while the instrument they are
copying reaches the emplaced case on one amended definition. And the
consensus objection is met on the record: the anti-vehicle-mines protocol
died post-stockpile in 2006, Protocol IV succeeded pre-deployment in 1995,
and emplaced systems are still in the second window - with CCW art 8, the
AP II Annual Conference, and the rolling text's own exclusion clause as the
standing routes.
