Line-by-line pass on the two GGE papers; the skip-path declared

Both papers were read line by line, in full, after yesterday's revision. The
read found what a fast revision leaves: the abstract and the closing remarks
still called the rolling text's core a prohibition two sections after the body
established it no longer is one; the migration point was argued twice in the
same words; the March session example sat in the middle of a three-limb
argument instead of after it; one internal footnote label leaked into print
as "n 703"; one Box citation survived; and the paper's own build caught the
repair and forced it into proper OSCOLA shape - full citation at first
mention, back-reference after. MINE lost a duplicated qualifier, an
unverifiable stockpile figure, and an overstatement of the ICRC's position
("exactly that pattern... the effective one" is now "among the most
effective", which is what the source says).

A reviewing agent assessed the structural worry - readers lost in technical
detail - and found the skip-architecture already present but undeclared: the
abstracts are the argument in brief, the headings narrate, every technical
stretch ends in a stated result. What was added is four sentences, not
summaries: each paper's roadmap now names the pressed reader's path (Part I,
the central section, the drafted text, the conclusions), and each Part III
tells the expert what they may pass over and where to rejoin. The drafted
texts, the falsification sections and the conclusions were left untouched.

Rebuilt through both pipelines: structural problems 0, citation checks 0,
apparatus renumbered, page counts unchanged at 34 and 25.
