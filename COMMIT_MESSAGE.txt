Phone HUD fold on the 3D demos: the scene owns the screen until you ask for the instruments

On a phone the robots demo buried its storeroom under nine overlapping
pills - scenario chips, an engagement log printed over them, two sensor
spec panels, a status line behind the Run button - and the launcher demo
covered its bottom half with the full control rack. Both viewports now
open clean on a small screen: the scene, a Run/Launch button, Reset, and
one pill - "Info".

Opening it restores everything. On the robots demo the log, the scenario
status and the controls flow BELOW the scene when open, because stacking
them back over the chips is what caused the original collision; the
scenario selector and sensor specs overlay the top, where there is sky to
spare. Closing folds it all away again. Desktop is untouched - the toggle
does not exist above 700px.

The other 3D pages were checked and left alone: the geomagnetic orbit
screen already keeps its legend below the canvas, and the slew and
explorer pages put three small chips on a sub-200px chart, which is not
the problem being solved.

Verified in a real browser at 390px: closed, open, closed again - state
and button label correct both ways, zero page errors - and at 1440px:
toggle absent, full HUD visible as before.
